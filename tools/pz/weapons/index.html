Difficulty scope: The named presets contain many other differences. For this comparator, only Zombie Toughness currently changes the neutral weapon calculations: it changes zombie HP distribution and the toughness-based melee crit modifier. Zombie Strength changes how hard zombies hit players/objects, not your weapon damage. Speed/sprinters, population, cognition, hearing, injuries, loot and respawn alter encounter difficulty or weapon availability, but do not directly alter the intrinsic damage/APS/endurance/reach/durability formulas under the healthy-player assumptions used here. Outbreak and Apocalypse therefore intentionally produce the same weapon calculations.

Probability model: One-shot chance is calculated deterministically from the weapon damage distribution, crit chance and selected zombie-health distribution—there is no per-weapon Monte Carlo noise in the 1-shot column. Multi-hit outcomes and expected hits/kill use common deterministic samples across all weapons to prevent arbitrary favorable rolls.

* Impact (s): Estimated time from starting a standard attack until the weapon connects with the zombie. Lower is faster/more responsive; it is different from APS, which measures sustained attack rate.

† Kills to break: Fresh-state estimate using expected hits-to-kill and overall-condition loss. The comparator assumes weapons remain sharp.

* Special-condition weapon behavior: An asterisk marks weapons with a KNIFE/Stab close-kill path. Close Kill / Jaw Stab requires special positioning/combat conditions and is excluded from the normal standing-vs-standing calculations. Rear attacks and prone-target attacks also have special modifiers and are excluded from the neutral baseline.

Repeated-hit damage: B42.20 tracks successive melee hits on a zombie. Hits 1–3 use normal damage; beginning with hit 4, the runtime applies its escalating hit-count modifier. This affects expected hits/kill and related sustained metrics, but not first-hit one-shot probability.

* Speed timing: APS, hit time and post-hit recovery use the traced B42.20 normal standing melee animation clips/events and runtime CombatSpeed. Forbidden Technique models the interact/grapple recovery technique by capping applicable post-attack MeleeDelay at 2 units; it does not shorten the attack animation or hit time.

Wpn vs Effective: Wpn Dmg and Wpn Crit show stock weapon-definition values. Eff. Dmg is the configured ordinary non-critical first-hit damage range. Eff. Crit is the configured ordinary crit chance; KNIFE/Stab attacks show 0%. Hover the combat-skill icon beside a weapon name to see its skill.
